Join Us

We are always excited to connect with curious, motivated scientists at the undergraduate, graduate, and postdoctoral levels. Our research spans protein biochemistry, chemical biology, microbiology, molecular biology, and bioinformatics, and we especially encourage scientists from diverse scientific and personal backgrounds to apply.

Before applying, we invite you to learn about our lab Culture. Please reach out to discuss research opportunities before submitting a formal application.

Undergraduate & Master's Students

We occasionally have openings for Northeastern undergraduate and master's students. Send your resume (relevant coursework, co-op experience, and any previous research) with a brief statement of your research interests.

Email

Prospective Graduate Students

Apply through the Chemistry or Biology PhD programs. Once admitted, you are welcome to rotate in our lab and join if it is a good fit for your graduate training.

Prospective Postdocs

Send your CV, a cover letter describing your academic background, research interests, goals for postdoctoral training, and contact information for three professional references.

Email
