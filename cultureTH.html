Culture

These values guide our approach to how we do science and how we work together.

Our Values

Curiosity

We encourage creative thinking, bold ideas, and the freedom to explore. Some of the best science comes from asking unexpected questions.

Scientific Rigor

We are committed to careful, reproducible, and high-quality science. We take the time to design experiments thoughtfully and interpret data objectively.

Integrity

We act with honesty and accountability. We give credit generously, acknowledge and learn from our mistakes, and treat everyone with respect.

Open Science

We share data, code, and preprints openly whenever possible. We encourage the broader scientific community to build on our work.

Collaboration

We collaborate within our group and across disciplines, institutions, and career stages. Different perspectives lead to better science.

Scientific Inclusivity

We foster a positive learning environment where scientists from all backgrounds feel welcome, respected, and supported. Diversity makes our science stronger.

Mentorship

We invest in the growth and success of every group member. We provide individualized mentorship, support career development, and celebrate the achievements of our team.

Well-Being

We work hard but believe sustainable science requires taking care of ourselves. We prioritize a safe lab, respect personal time, and support the mental and physical well-being of our scientists.

Want to know more about how we work? Read our Lab Compact, which describes our shared expectations and commitments in detail.

Outreach

Making science accessible and exciting to general audiences is as important as training the next generation of scientists. We believe scientists have a responsibility to inform, engage, and inspire the public. Outreach humanizes scientists, builds public support for science, and empowers people to think critically and make informed decisions.
